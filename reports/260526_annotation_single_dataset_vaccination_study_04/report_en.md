## Important Limitation

This 260526 report is an intermediate review generated from v11 evidence containers. For four datasets, the upstream workflow first reduced the original gene space to 8,000 pre-HVG genes and then to an approximately 4,000-gene analysis X/HVG space. Therefore, marker-gene availability alerts in this report mean absence from the v11 analysis container, not necessarily absence from the original data. Markers such as FOXP3/IL2RA/CTLA4 are present in the original processed H5AD for several datasets but were dropped from the analysis container. Final submission-quality interpretation requires regenerating CellTypist, marker scoring, and marker availability from the original all-gene input.

## Dataset-Specific Interpretation

- `vaccination_study_04`: 66,065 cells, 16,983 original processed genes, 8,000 pre-HVG genes, 3,971 analysis X/var genes, 11 submitted labels, parent/Blood residual fraction 0.008, median confidence 0.845.
  - 1,782 cells have low confidence and should be inspected on QC/confidence UMAPs.
  - 1,249 cells are submitted as `Doublet`; inspect mixed-lineage marker expression before final submission.
  - 531 cells remain `Blood Cell`; these are residual ambiguous cells rather than filtered cells.
  - Marker availability alerts are present for: CD4_naive_tcm, CD4_effector_memory, Treg, B_naive, B_memory_ABC, Plasma_ASC. Fine labels relying on these marker sets should be treated cautiously.

## Dataset-Specific Assessment

- Overall: 66,065 cells / 16,983 original processed genes / 8,000 pre-HVG genes / 3,971 analysis X/var genes. The composition is strongly monocyte/DC-enriched: Classical Monocyte 34,105 cells, Non-Classical Monocyte 15,314 cells, cDC2 7,694 cells, and pDC 5,447 cells. This should be read as a myeloid/DC-enriched dataset rather than balanced whole PBMC.
- More reliable regions: Myeloid/DC labels show low disagreement: Classical Monocyte 0.031, Non-Classical Monocyte 0.008, cDC1 0.003, and pDC 0.019. Myeloid/DC annotation is relatively stable here.
- Caveat: T/B marker availability is broadly poor, with alerts for CD4_naive_tcm, CD4_effector_memory, Treg, B_naive, B_memory_ABC, and Plasma_ASC. Actual T/B counts are very small, so those fine labels should not be overinterpreted.
- Doublet/parent residual: Doublet is 1,249 cells and Blood Cell is 531 cells. Parent/Blood residual fraction is acceptable at 0.008, but ambiguous cells should be checked for UMAP localization in the myeloid-dominant structure.
- Interpretation: This is primarily an input-composition issue, not a global annotation failure. Submitted labels are most reliable for myeloid/DC populations; T/B fine labels are auxiliary because of marker missingness and low cell counts.
