## Dataset-Specific Assessment

- Overall: 57,419 cells / 11,878 original processed genes / 11,878 pre-HVG genes / 11,878 analysis X/var genes. Gene coverage is the highest among the tested datasets, but source disagreement is also the highest at 22,047 cells (0.384), and median confidence is lower at 0.767.
- Composition: CD4 Naive/Tcm 30,218 cells, CD8 Cytotoxic/Tem 10,674 cells, NK Cell 9,440 cells, and Memory B Cell 3,212 cells dominate. Myeloid labels are very small and should not be overinterpreted.
- Main weakness: Memory B Cell disagreement is 0.792, indicating strong CellTypist/Pan-human/marker tension in B-cell fine labels. CD4/CD8 T labels also show around 0.39 disagreement and need marker-expression review inside T subclusters.
- Marker availability: Treg lacks FOXP3 and Plasma_ASC lacks JCHAIN and other ASC markers. Even with high total gene count, key marker availability remains label-specific.
- Interpretation: Broad T/NK/B lineage structure is present, but fine-state assignment has high reference disagreement. The next improvement should strengthen marker-margin and source-agreement rules inside T/B subclusters, with confidence caps for low-margin fine labels.
